Lucent Raw: what is built and what is not

Made on 2026-10-23 from features.csv, the one list that is kept up to date as the program is built. It is meant to be honest: "Built" means it works and is tested; "Partly built" means it works but something a person would expect is missing, and the note says what; "Not built" means it does not exist; "Left out on purpose" means it was decided not to build it.

Totals: 146 features. Built: 75. Partly built: 50. Not built: 5. Left out on purpose: 16.

The feature list is made from the program being copied (Lightroom Classic), so it counts that program's features. Some things in Lucent Raw are not in that program at all (the B BOT advisor, the AI connection, AI enlargement and the AI Fill spot mode); they appear in the notes and are not counted in the score. Parity with the original, scored as the build requires (must-haves count 3, should 2, could 1, partly built counts half, left-out features not counted), was 41.3 out of 100 on 18 October 2026. See parity.md.

What this list cannot tell you

  • Tested is not the same as proven on real use. The core has 306 automated tests and the interface 359, but no automated test drives the real window with the real core, and most features have been tried on test pictures rather than on a photographer's real library. The first real use will find things.
  • No installer exists yet, and the program has only ever been run on the machine it was built on.
  • RAW support has been tried on a small number of camera files. Other makers and very new cameras may not work.
  • A licence question is open (the RAW-reading library is LGPL-2.1) and must be settled before the program is shared or sold. See THIRD_PARTY.md.

Lens blur (depth)Partly builtDepth Anything V2 Small (Apache licence, downloaded with your approval, built in, runs offline) measures how near each part of the photo is. About 2.5 s to measure a photo. Rust tested (the model on a made-up scene, the depth range, the blur under a mask). Photo merge HDRPartly builtPhoto > Merge to HDR…: 2 to 9 selected photos of one scene at different exposures are merged by exposure fusion (weights from contrast, colour and exposure; blended at several scales so there are no seams), lined up first if asked (a shift found from median-threshold bitmaps), and the result is a JPEG beside the first photo, added to the catalog and selected. Rust tested with made-up shots (no clipped shadows or highlights, an exact 3,-2 pixel realignment). Not built: a result with a wider range than 8 bits (it is a finished picture, not a DNG), ghost removal, a merge in the Develop module, panorama merge. Rust tested with made-up shots (the place found exactly, a joined picture that matches the scene, a refusal with words when photos do not overlap). The simple form only: no bending (no spherical or cylindrical projection), no rotation or perspective, no filling of the edges; tilted or hand-turned shots will show seams. Not tried on real panorama shots.

Catalog
